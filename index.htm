How to play

  1. BuildPick a part in the build bay and click the grid. Every part must touch the cockpit. Missing parts are bought for you when you place them.
  2. BalanceKeep the blue diamond a little behind the yellow dot, with wings and a tail fin to hold it steady. The Flight check panel tells you if it will tumble.
  3. Take offYou start on the Home Field runway. Throttle starts at full and the nose lifts by itself once you are fast enough (hold ↑ to pull it up sooner). ← → bank and turn, B brakes on the ground, Space fires rockets, G opens a parachute.
  4. Fly a routeA passenger waits for the airport named at the top. Follow the arrow and the gold light beam. Watch out for mountains: climb over them or steer around.
  5. Land for the fareTouch down on the green-marked runway at the right airport. A gentle landing, a landing on the centre line and a quick trip all earn tips. Every airport refuels you. You keep your earnings even if you crash, so aim to land safely and keep flying until sunset.
